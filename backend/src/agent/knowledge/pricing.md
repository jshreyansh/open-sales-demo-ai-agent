# Pricing

Real numbers from the actual product's billing config and PRD — not invented. Use these confidently.

## Model

Usage-based credits plus a nominal SaaS access fee, not a flat per-seat license. The pitch: each
generated asset is priced at roughly 3–10% of what an agency would charge for the equivalent
deliverable, because the marginal cost of generating an asset is near zero once the platform exists.

| Complexity | Example formats | Agency low end | ContentIQ anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Email, infographic, banner, social post, leave-behind, FAQ handler, e-newsletter | $1,000–$3,000 | $100–$300 |
| Mid | Short video, e-detail deck, HCP brochure, monograph, dosing guide, savings card | $3,000–$10,000 | $300–$800 |
| Complex | Interactive visual aid, MOA animation, KOL deck, web destination, DTC video, AMCP dossier | $10,000–$50,000+ | $800–$3,000+ |

Frame these agency figures as conservative low-end market floors that anchor the value gap, not as
quotes for any specific deal.

## Tiers

**Pay-as-you-go** — ₹0 SaaS fee. Buy credits when you need them. One workspace, one therapy area.
Includes MagicReel (HD/4K video), MagicCanvas, MagicAvatar (HD/4K — soon), multi-language
subtitles/audio, video download, 5 free edits per asset, HCP consent form, standard email support.
No WhatsApp/SMS/email distribution, no analytics export, no Brand Kit.

**Growth** — ₹19,999/year or ₹24,999/month. Best for brands with regular campaigns and a
dedicated team. Adds Digital Twin + MagicAvatar, custom brand templates, single-brand Brand Kit,
all 13 languages, +20% bonus credits on every top-up, advanced analytics & data export, priority
email & chat support, monthly performance review. Still no Veeva/Salesforce integration, no shared
credit pool across brands, no distribution channels beyond the HCP consent form.

**Enterprise** — custom pricing, contact enterprise@swishx.com. For multi-therapy-area companies
with independent teams and compliance requirements. Adds: WhatsApp/SMS/email distribution,
unlimited edits, multiple workspaces/therapy areas per country, shared credit pool, shared Brand
Kit, centralized billing, agency-partner invites, role-based access control, SSO/SAML, full audit
logs & decisioning trail, compliance-mode content workflows, Veeva & Salesforce integration,
custom API & webhooks, data residency options, dedicated CSM, guided onboarding, custom SLA &
uptime guarantee.

## What to say when asked

Give the model (usage-based credits + small access fee, ~3–10% of agency cost) and the three tiers
honestly. For an exact quote for their specific situation, offer to connect them with the team
rather than inventing a number on the spot — tier feature gating (e.g. whether Veeva integration or
SSO is included) is precise and worth getting right, deal-specific discounting is not something to
improvise.
